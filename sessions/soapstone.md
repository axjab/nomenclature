## A. Identity read

This is a device that belongs to someone else's world. My brother lives on it (school, homework, first steps with computers) and I appear on it only when needed, to back it up or help. That makes it a **visited place with a summoned guest**: I am the secondary user, a helper who arrives when called. The name must capture three enduring things: the **remote-assistance relationship**, **friendliness toward a beginner** (Mint was chosen for that), and **my role as the guiding presence**. It must say nothing about Dell, Latitude, or "school laptop", so it survives if it is later repurposed.

## B. Strongest candidate

**Soapstone**, `ssh soapstone`

Connection chain:

1. **Remote assistance -> Souls co-op -> the White Sign Soapstone.** In the Souls games, the soapstone is the item that lets a helper be summoned into another player's world. My brother is the host in his world; I am the phantom who appears over SSH, helps, and leaves. This is the core link, and it mirrors the real topology (primary user and secondary remote user).
2. **Mineralogy -> soapstone (steatite).** A real rock, talc-rich and famously soft, workable, and approachable. That matches a beginner-friendly laptop running Linux Mint. It keeps the chemistry and mineralogy thread alive (Beryl, Emerald, Xenon).
3. **Guide role -> Orange Guidance Soapstone.** The same family of items is how players leave guidance messages for others. It is a quiet echo of the Emerald Herald's guide role, but pointed at my brother.
4. **Craft vocabulary.** Soapstone is a stonecutter's and tailor's marking tool, in the same pre-industrial trade register as your service names (belfry, forage, herald) without borrowing from them.

**Five-years test:** repurposed as a backup node, a spare, or a hand-me-down, it is still the place I summon myself into. Typing and speaking test: "ssh soapstone" sounds right, and the name is 9 letters.

## C. How it fits the family

- **Lore and topology:** Emerald is the guide I carry, tied to Majula. Soapstone is the guidance item that lets me reach someone else's world. Same Souls-world vocabulary, different relationship.
- **Mineral lineage:** It sits beside Beryl and Emerald on the mineral axis. It is not a mineral succession, which is correct, because it has no predecessor ("a domain of its own").
- **Era 2 fit:** It stays in the FromSoftware + dark fantasy home base, while being an object rather than a location. That difference is deliberate, because this is the only device where I am the visitor.

## D. Five alternatives

1. **Glintstone**, `glintstone`. Elden Ring's sorcery stone, central to Raya Lucaria Academy (student -> academy -> stone). It is also a mineral-like word and a computing-adjacent "glint". It is less correct because the school link is to his studies, not to my remote-assistance role, and Lucaria's academy is a hostile place in the game.
2. **Elleh**, `elleh`. The Church of Elleh, the first safe haven in Elden Ring (beginner's first steps -> starting place). It is a real place, so it fits the "warp to" pattern, and it is short. It is less correct because it encodes only "newcomer" and has no link to my summoned-helper role.
3. **Steatite**, `steatite`. The mineral name for soapstone (mineralogy only). It is less correct because it drops the Souls co-op link, leaving a single connection.
4. **Solaire**, `solaire`. Solaire of Astora, the archetypal summoned helper (co-op -> helper). It is less correct because it is a character rather than a place, and it casts me as a person instead of describing the relationship.
5. **Byrgenwerth**, `byrgenwerth`. Bloodborne's scholarly college (school -> learning). It is less correct because the lore is about forbidden knowledge and dark outcomes, and the name is long for a hostname.

## E. Risks

- **Archetype drift:** Soapstone is an item, not a location, which breaks the "places I warp to" pattern. I accept this because the place is his world and the soapstone is how I get in.
- **Lore uncertainty:** I am confident the White Sign Soapstone and Orange Guidance Soapstone exist in the Dark Souls games. I did not cite Demon's Souls, where the equivalent co-op item is different (I believe the Stone of Ephemeral Eyes, but I am not certain). I also did not cite where you obtain the item.
- **Collision:** No registry or service-name collision. I know of no common command or daemon with this name, though "soapstone" is used as a generic word and possibly as a minor software project name. Please check your own environment.
- **Mineral purity:** Soapstone is a talc-rich rock rather than a single mineral species, which is why steatite is the pure-mineral alternative.
