
**A. Identity read**

This is a carried workhorse: a daily driver that goes home to work and back, docks over USB-C, and is built to be used hard. It's your first pure development machine, so it's a craftsman's tool and not a companion in the Emerald sense. It also marks the shift from gaming to building, and it inherits two lineages: Luminous (dev duty) and Xenon (daily-driver duty). The name has to say "this is where things get made" and nod to the light-themed predecessors. It also has to survive the machine being repurposed.

**B. Strongest candidate: Titanite (`titanite`)**

Connection chain:
1. **Craft and upgrade (archetype):** In Dark Souls, titanite is the material you carry to a blacksmith to make your gear stronger. A carried development machine is the thing you improve your craft with, and it's the upgrade over what came before. That fits the tool/craftsman archetype.
2. **FromSoftware:** Titanite appears throughout Dark Souls (shards, chunks, slabs, scales). It's the currency of the blacksmiths at Firelink Shrine (Andre) and Majula (Lenigrast).
3. **Mineralogy:** Titanite is a real mineral (formerly called sphene). That's the same lane as Beryl and Emerald.
4. **Light nod to Luminous and Xenon:** Titanite is prized as a gem for its dispersion, the "fire" that splits light into colours, which exceeds diamond's. That's a real, quiet link to Luminous and Xenon's light and lamp associations.
5. **Durability:** The mineral is a titanium calcium silicate, and titanium stands for toughness. That matches the business-class build, though I'm treating it as identity and not as a spec.

**C. How it fits the family**

- It bridges your two eras: a mineral (Era 1 and the Beryl/Emerald lane) that is also a Dark Souls item (Era 2). A new-era device that belongs to both is a good fit.
- Lore echoes your topology: you carry titanite to the blacksmiths at Firelink and Majula, so the laptop in your bag is what gets taken to your VPS and home server. That mirrors how you actually work.
- It sits beside `soapstone` as another Dark Souls item you carry, which makes the two laptops feel like a pair without sharing a suffix.
- It's a fresh name that nods to Luminous through light and to the chemistry lane through the mineral. It isn't numbered and doesn't reuse an old name.

**D. Alternatives, ranked**

1. **Tungsten** (`tungsten`). Xenon arc lamps use tungsten electrodes, and tungsten is also the lamp filament metal, so it nods to Xenon and Luminous. It's a hard, durable metal used for cutting tools, and it's chemistry-lane like the Xenon era. It has no FromSoftware link, so it's a two-link name where Titanite has four.
2. **Andre** (`andre`). Andre of Astora is the Firelink blacksmith, and it's a clean craftsman fit with a lore tie to Firelink. It has no predecessor nod and no mineral link, and a bare person's name reads as a label.
3. **Sunstone** (`sunstone`). It's a real mineral, it's light-related for the Luminous nod, and it pairs with `soapstone`. The Norse navigation-stone legend is a possible mythology link, but I'm unsure of its details. It has no craft or work link and no firm FromSoftware tie.
4. **Lenigrast** (`lenigrast`). He's the Majula blacksmith, which ties it to your home server. It's obscure, awkward to say aloud, and has no predecessor nod, so the connections are thin.
5. **Adamant** (`adamant`). It's the mythic unbreakable substance (the root of "diamond"), which suits business-class durability and carries a sense of resolve. It has no tie to existing names and no FromSoftware link.

**E. Risks**

- **Lore:** I'm fairly confident Andre is the Firelink blacksmith and Lenigrast is the Majula one, and that titanite is the upgrade material in both. Please verify the exact blacksmith locations before relying on them. The chain doesn't depend on them, since titanite alone carries the Dark Souls link.
- **Collisions:** No known command or daemon is called titanite. It's loosely close to Titanium and Titan products, but nothing you'd confuse it with.
- **Weakness:** The "upgrade" reading is interpretive, since titanite improves things and doesn't hold them. If it feels too gear-centric, `tungsten` is the safer chemistry-only choice.
- **Typing:** It's 8 letters, but `tita` plus tab works.
