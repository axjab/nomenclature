=== 1. HOW I NAME (evidence, not rules) ===
My philosophy: a name must be deeply meaningful to the device's identity AND carry high aesthetic, poetic, or atmospheric resonance as a concrete noun, active tool, or physical entity.

Patterns you must detect and continue:
- Multi-link convergence: strong names sit on 2-4 independent connections (Emerald has four). One connection is a label; several is a name.
- Identity over hardware: names never encode specs, models, or current tasks.
- Replacement = fresh name with a nod: when a device replaces another, it gets a NEW name that acknowledges its predecessor through a real connection (Beryl -> Emerald: same mineral family). Do not number successors.
- Lore and world mirror real topology: relationships between names echo real relationships between devices (Emerald Herald lives in Majula = my phone is tied to my home server).
- Conceptual Era Bridges: When moving into a new era (e.g., from gaming/lore to dev/creation), the name should act as a bridge—taking a motif from the previous era (fire/light/elements) and recasting it into the new era's metaphor (creation, forge, workspace).
- THE SWEET SPOT FILTER (CRITICAL):
  Look at Xenon, Luminous, Firelink, Majula, and Catalyst. They are NOT dry textbook nouns (Prism, Plumbline), abstract concepts (Arcanum), or obscure fantasy appendices (Silmaril, Athanor). They feel like legendary places, high-concept sci-fi entities, sleek arcane phenomena, or equippable instruments of power.
  Target vibe benchmarks: Catalyst, Crucible, Halcyon, Aegis, Cinder, Solace, Astra, Sunweaver.

Axes to draw from (strongest names intersect several):
- Fire, Light, Arcane & Optical Phenomena: Radiant energy, embers, refraction, light-and-dark duality (lineage to Luminous, Xenon, and Firelink).
- Chemical, Mineral & Physical Concepts: Noble elements, crystal lattices, fundamental forces, catalysts, gem tones (lineage to Xenon, Beryl, Emerald).
- Iconic High-Fantasy / Gaming Topography & Equipment: Universally recognizable hubs, fires, sanctuaries, or equippable sorcery focuses/catalysts (Dark Souls, Elden Ring, broader mythos).
- Creation, Code-as-Sorcery & Unix Daemons: Software development on Linux is modern spellcraft—typing precise syntax into terminals to compile logic and manage background Unix daemons. The device is the active instrument where this invocation happens.
- Dual-Domain Convergence: Prioritize words that sit at the exact intersection of Real Scientific/Technical Systems (chemistry, physics, mechanics) AND Dark Fantasy Sorcery/Equipment (FromSoft focuses, spellcraft tools).

=== 2. HARD CONSTRAINTS ===
- Part of Speech: NOUNS, COMPOUND NOUNS, TITLES, AND PROPER NOUNS ONLY. Verbs, action words, gerunds, or imperative commands are STRICTLY FORBIDDEN (NO kindle, spark, run, focus-as-verb).
- Object Type Rules:
  1. NO ABSTRACT CONCEPTS (Arcanum, Knowledge)—must be a physical, tangible object, vessel, active tool, or place.
  2. NO PASSIVE CHARMS (Talisman, Aegis)—must represent an ACTIVE, transforming tool of execution.
- Hostname: lowercase a-z ONLY. No hyphens, no digits, no spaces, no punctuation. One word.
- Length: 5-8 letters preferred (up to 9 for clean compound hostnames). 2-3 syllables max.
- Always provide BOTH full name ("Firelink Shrine") and typed hostname (firelink).
- Speakable: 100% intuitive pronunciation and spelling on the first try.
- STRICTLY BANNED:
  1. Verbs, action words, gerunds, or terminal command verbs.
  2. Abstract non-physical nouns (Arcanum, Logic, Synthesis).
  3. Dry corporate/stock tech nouns (Prism, Zenith, Matrix, Core, Laptop).
  4. Obscure high-fantasy proper nouns requiring lore deep-dives (Silmaril, Eärendil).
  5. Archaic/academic Latin or alchemy jargon (Athanor, Azoth, Plumbline).

=== 4. PROCESS (do this silently before answering) ===
1. Extract the device's enduring identity: daily-driver development workhorse, carrying companion, bridge from gaming/light to pure code/creation.
2. Grammar & Object Type Check: Is this word strictly a noun or proper noun? Is it a physical, tangible object or active tool rather than an abstract concept or passive charm? If verb or abstract, REJECT IT.
3. Dual-Domain & Convergence Check: Does it bridge scientific/physical reality with dark fantasy spellcraft/lore? Does it connect code syntax with Unix daemons?
4. Sweet Spot Filter: Does this feel as iconic and atmospheric as Majula, Luminous, or Catalyst without sounding like a textbook?
5. Run the speakable/typing test ("ssh into ___"). Check for Linux command collisions (e.g., `cat` vs `catalyst`).

=== 5. OUTPUT FORMAT ===
A. Identity read: 3-5 lines on the archetype and what the name captures.
B. Strongest candidate: name, hostname, and the connection chain (listing independent connections).
C. How it fits the family: ties to Luminous, Xenon, Majula, Emerald, etc.
D. Five alternatives, ranked, each with name, hostname, connections, and why it ranked lower.
E. Risks: collisions or weaknesses.
F. Registry line: ready to paste.
